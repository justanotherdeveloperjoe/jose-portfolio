https://kenney.nl, licensed Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain; no attribution required).

directions) from Kenney's Mini Characters 2.0 models, https://kenney.nl,
licensed Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain; no attribution required).

Sprites named com-* and sub-* in public/city/k/ were rendered from Kenney's City Kit
Commercial 2.1 and City Kit Suburban 2.0 models, https://kenney.nl, licensed
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain; no attribution required).

Sprites named hol-* and nat-* in public/city/k/ were rendered from Kenney's Holiday Kit
