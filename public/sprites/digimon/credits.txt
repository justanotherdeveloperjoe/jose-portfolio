Preparation
Only the small overworld frames are used. Source backgrounds were keyed
out, and frames were placed in 56px cells with a shared ground anchor.
The original character pixels are displayed at an integer 3x scale.
Original ripper credits are preserved here rather than in the game viewport.

Animation notes
Walking and idle playback use original frame sequences. Dedicated eating,
sleeping, training, and affectionate reaction strips were not available in
the selected sheets. Those activities reuse existing poses with text cues.
The short Digivolution fade is implemented in CSS, not a ripped game effect.

The Monterrey city illustration is reused from this portfolio's original
postcard. Pet logic and interface are implemented locally without copied
third-party game-engine code. No remote sprite service is needed at runtime.
